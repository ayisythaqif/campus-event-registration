# Problem Statement

## Who is affected?
Students, campus event organizers, and university administrators are affected by the current event registration process.

## What exactly is going wrong?
Students may have difficulty finding information about campus events and registering for them when registration is handled manually or through different platforms. Organizers may struggle to track registrations, manage participant lists, and identify available spaces.

## Why does it matter?
These problems can lead to missed events, duplicate registrations, inaccurate participant records, and wasted time for both students and organizers.

## Problem statement (3 to 5 sentences)
Students, campus event organizers, and university administrators face challenges managing campus event registrations. Manual registration processes and scattered event information make it difficult for students to discover events and register easily. Organizers may experience duplicate registrations, inaccurate participant lists, and difficulties tracking attendance. A Campus Event Registration System is needed to centralize event information, simplify registration, and improve the overall management of campus events.
